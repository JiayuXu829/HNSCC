# RADCURE / ORCESTRA license and citation notes

**Audit date:** 2026-08-07  
**Status:** usable for research with attribution; artifact terms remain source-controlled.

- TCIA lists the RADCURE Version 4 clinical XLSX (3,346 subjects) under **CC BY 4.0** and requires the dataset citation with DOI `10.7937/J47W-NM11`.
- The downloaded CT/DICOM/RTSTRUCT archives were deliberately excluded; TCIA lists those imaging materials under controlled-access rules.
- Zenodo record `10.5281/zenodo.14226536` lists `RADCURE_READII-RADIOMICS_MAE.RDS` as **CC BY 4.0**. The downloaded MD5 matches the publisher value.
- Redistribution or publication must credit the creators and source, identify changes, and retain the accession/DOI.
- This file is a provenance note, not legal advice. Re-check the source pages before public redistribution.
