**Status:** public research use documented; preserve artifact-level caution.

- The external FAU ZIP files used here do not embed a standalone license file. Therefore, treat structured tables, official splits, and TMA density measurements as citation-required source artifacts and re-confirm portal terms before redistributing copies.
