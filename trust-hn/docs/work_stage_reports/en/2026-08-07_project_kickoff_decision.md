# Decision 0001: staged TRUST-HN implementation

**Date:** 2026-08-07  
**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

The master protocol is authoritative. Work will stop at every scientific gate rather than moving directly from data discovery to model fitting. The first execution block is limited to Phase 0 and Phase 1.

## Rationale

The central claim depends more on endpoint fidelity, patient-level splitting, shortcut controls, and sealed evaluation than on model complexity. Premature modeling would risk outcome leakage and invalidate the primary comparison.

## Consequences

- The three studies remain separate clinical/ecological settings.
- RADCURE locked outcomes, HANCOCK OOD outcomes, and GEO external outcomes are not used for tuning.
- Dataset names and population claims remain provisional until histology/index-date audits are complete.
- Phase 2 is not authorized until a written go/no-go review.
