1. A shrinkage-controlled residual set-survival backbone improved 24-month discrimination over the V0 clinical-pathological anchor in repeated nested cross-fitting.
2. Mean Uno C improved from 0.644239 to 0.665542 (+0.021303), and all five repetition seeds improved in the same direction.
3. Mean 24-month time-dependent AUC improved from 0.662022 to 0.682511 (+0.020488).
4. Mean IPCW Brier score improved from 0.124745 to 0.124338 (delta -0.000407).
5. The model retained 100% prediction coverage and exact clinical fallback when no usable optional modality was available.
6. The worst supported-pattern Brier regret was +0.009848, remaining inside the prespecified +0.020 safety boundary.
7. The positive interpretation is that optional multimodal measurements can add incremental prognostic information when introduced as a shrinkage-controlled residual rather than an unconstrained replacement of clinical risk.

These claims are development-stage claims. Confirmation, transportability and clinical utility require a separately frozen outcome-untouched evaluation.
