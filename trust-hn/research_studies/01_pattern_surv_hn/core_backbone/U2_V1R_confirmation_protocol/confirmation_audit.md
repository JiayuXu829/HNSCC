# U2/V1R locked confirmation audit

- The researcher-confirmed HANCOCK OOD cohort was designated outcome-untouched before prediction generation.
- Twenty-five matched V0/V1R members were fit only on development training partitions.
- Prediction, code, dependency, input and protocol hashes were sealed before outcomes were read.
- Confirmation outcomes were then unsealed once, and the frozen estimands plus 2,000 stratified patient bootstraps were run without refitting, calibration, routing or patient removal.
- This result is confirmation evidence for the frozen V1R rescue protocol; it does not convert the original V1 decision into a preregistered pass or establish clinical utility.
