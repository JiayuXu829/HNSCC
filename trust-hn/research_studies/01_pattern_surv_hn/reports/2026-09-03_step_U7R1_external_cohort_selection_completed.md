# U7R1 external cohort selection completed

**Date:** 2026-09-03  
**Status:** RADCURE selected for secondary external characterization; formal current-V1R confirmation remains blocked

RADCURE was selected because it is the largest available independent cohort (626 patients, 110 events) and has clinical plus radiomics data. However, it lacks the frozen current-V1R blood/ICD/TMA input contract and is not certified outcome-untouched in the current inventory. It cannot therefore be used to claim formal current-V1R external validation.

The closest technically compatible cohort, HANCOCK OOD, has already been used for the locked raw-V1R confirmation and post-unseal bridge characterization, so it cannot be reused as a new pristine external confirmation.

This step generated no current-V1R predictions or external metrics. Before any RADCURE analysis, a separate explicitly exploratory protocol must be frozen that names the adapted comparator and forbids modality substitution, outcome-dependent tuning and promotion to current-V1R evidence.

## Paper implication

RADCURE may support a separate external characterization subsection about transportability under a different clinical/radiomics contract. It cannot complete the paper's current-V1R external-validation claim. The last completed efficacy result remains the locked raw-V1R directional confirmation in HANCOCK OOD.
